# Colour scheme for well borders

A colour scheme for the borders of wells that contains 4 colours. The
colours are meant to mark the status of a well (Fail, Check, OK and Not
tested). They do not appear in the `protti_colours` and
`viridis_colours` schemes, which ensures that borders are visible on
every fill colour.

## Usage

``` r
border_colours
```

## Format

A vector containing 4 colours
